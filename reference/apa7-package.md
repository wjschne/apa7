# apa7: Writing Documents in American Psychological Association Style, Seventh Edition

Create tables and format statistics in the style of the Publication
Manual of the American Psychological Association, Seventh Edition.
Functions turn fitted models and data into APA-style tables (regression,
mixed, multinomial, count, and generalized additive models; ANOVA;
hypothesis tests; descriptive statistics; correlations; reliability;
factor analysis; structural equation models; estimated marginal means;
and meta-analyses), write their table notes, and extend 'flextable' for
tables made by hand.
